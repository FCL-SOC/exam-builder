# Using Claude with Exam Assistant

Claude can write a whole exam for you, or change one question, and you watch it happen in Exam Assistant.
It works in Claude Desktop, at school.

## Set up (once, about a minute)

1. Open **Claude Desktop**.
2. Double-click **exam-assistant.mcpb** (from IT or the shared drive).
3. Type your **three-letter staff code**, the one you use in Exam Assistant, and click **Install**.

## Use it

Put Claude on one side of your screen and Exam Assistant on the other, then just ask:

- *"Write a 40-mark Year 10 Maths test on quadratics, 50 minutes, scientific calculator. Section A multiple
  choice, Section B short answer."*
- *"In my Year 9 Science test, make question 4 harder and worth 3 marks."*
- *"Add two questions on completing the square after question 6."*
- *"Change the cover to Semester 2, and allow CAS calculators."*

For a new exam, Claude gives you a link: open it once. After that, Claude's changes appear in your editor within a
couple of seconds, and the changed questions flash yellow.

## Good to know

- **You can keep editing while Claude works.** If you both change the same question at once, *your* version is
  kept, and the bar at the top tells you.
- **To undo Claude's change,** press **Undo** in the editor, or tell Claude *"undo that"*.
- **Pictures:** Claude can't add images. It will say where one should go; drag the image onto the question.
- **Always read it through before printing.** Check answers and marks, especially the correct options in multiple
  choice (shown in green in the editor, never printed).
- **Only at school:** the extension only works on the school network.
- If Claude says your **staff code isn't set,** open Claude Desktop's **Settings → Extensions → Exam Assistant**.
